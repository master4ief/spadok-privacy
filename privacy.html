Privacy Policy

Velvra: ASMR Personalised Companion · Last updated: 27 May 2026

Velvra is an AI voice companion app. We respect your privacy. This policy explains what data the app handles and how. Velvra has no backend server — your conversations and profile are stored only on your device.

Data we handle

  • Email address — entered during onboarding. Stored only on your device; it is never transmitted to us or any server.
  • First name & date of birth — used to personalise your companion. Stored only on your device.
  • Chat messages — stored only on your device.

Third-party services

To generate replies and voice, the text of your messages and the companion's replies is sent to:

  • Google Gemini API — generates the companion's text replies. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • ElevenLabs API — converts reply text into speech. See ElevenLabs' Privacy Policy.

Your email, name and date of birth are not sent to these services.

Storage & retention

All profile data and chat history live locally on your device (no cloud sync). Uninstalling the app removes this data.

Data deletion

You can delete your account and all local data at any time via Settings → Delete account.

Selling data

We do not sell your data to third parties.

Children

Velvra is not directed to children and is not intended for use by them.

Medical disclaimer

Velvra is an AI companion, not a medical, psychological, or crisis service. It cannot provide medical advice. In a crisis, please seek help from a qualified professional or a local emergency service.
